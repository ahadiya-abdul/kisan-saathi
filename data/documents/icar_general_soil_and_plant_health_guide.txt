Beneficial Microorganisms, Bio-Fertilizers, and Soil Microbiome Restoration Guidelines

1. BENEFICIAL BIO-AGENTS FOR PLANT DEFENSE:
- Pseudomonas fluorescens: Acts as an antagonist against bacterial wilt, root rot, and damping-off fungi through secondary metabolite production (phenazines, 2,4-DAPG) and Induced Systemic Resistance (ISR). Foliar spray @ 5 g/L or soil drench @ 10 g/L.
- Bacillus subtilis: Colonizes plant roots, promotes vigor, solubilizes phosphorus, and suppresses fungal pathogens.

2. INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT (IPM) PRINCIPLES:
- Economic Injury Level (EIL) and Economic Threshold Level (ETL): Action should be taken when pest populations reach ETL, before reaching EIL.
- Bio-agents should be introduced early at egg or first-instar nymph/larval stage.
- Preserve resident natural enemies (ladybird beetles, hoverflies, predatory mites, rove beetles, spiders).
- Zero Residue Guarantee: Bio-agents leave no chemical residues in soil, water tables, or produce, qualifying crops for APMC organic market premiums.
