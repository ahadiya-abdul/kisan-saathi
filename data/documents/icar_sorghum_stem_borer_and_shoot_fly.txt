ICAR - Indian Institute of Millets Research (IIMR)
Biocontrol and Eco-Friendly IPM Protocol for Sorghum Stem Borer and Shoot Fly

1. TARGET PESTS:
- Sorghum Shoot Fly (Atherigona soccata): Attacks seedlings from 7 to 28 days after emergence, causing dead hearts in main tillers.
- Spotted Stem Borer (Chilo partellus): Attacks sorghum from 20 days after emergence until grain filling. Larvae feed in leaf whorls causing pinholes, then bore into stalks resulting in stem lodging and peduncle breakage.

2. BIOLOGICAL PARASITOIDS AND PREDATORS:
- Trichogramma chilonis: Release @ 50,000 parasitoids per acre at 20, 30, and 40 days after crop emergence. Staple Trichocards on lower leaf surface.
- Larval Parasitoid Bracon brevicornis / Bracon hebetor: Release @ 100 mated pairs per acre when stem borer larval activity is noted in the field.
- Pupal Parasitoid: Conserve Tetrastichus howardi, a natural gregarious pupal parasitoid of Chilo partellus.

3. MICROBIAL AND BOTANICAL SPRAYS:
- Spray Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) kurstaki formulation @ 2 g/L directed into the central leaf whorls at 25 and 35 days after emergence.
- Spray 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or Neem Azadirachtin 0.03% (300 ppm) @ 5 ml/L at first appearance of whorl feeding pinholes.

4. CULTURAL INTEGRATION:
- Intercropping: Intercrop Sorghum with Cowpea or Pigeon Pea in 4:2 ratio. Cowpea serves as a trap crop and refuge for beneficial predatory spiders and carabid ground beetles.
- High seed rate (1.2x) with thinning of shoot fly dead-heart seedlings at 12-14 DAE.
